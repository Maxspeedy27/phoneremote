How it works

Connected in under a minute

No phone app, no sign-up, no Bluetooth pairing menus. Install once on the laptop and you're done.

  1. 1

    Install on your laptop

    Download the installer for Windows, macOS or Linux and open Kumpas. A control panel with a QR code appears.

  2. 2

    Scan with your phone's camera

    The remote opens in your phone's browser: iPhone or Android, Safari or Chrome. Bookmark it or add it to your home screen.

  3. 3
    482 913482 913

    Approve the matching code

    Both screens show the same 6-digit code. Click Allow on the laptop. Your phone is remembered for next time.

Features

Everything a clicker does, and a lot it can't

Built for presenting, and just as handy on the sofa with a movie on the laptop.

Slides

Big Next and Back buttons, or just swipe. Start and end the show, black or white out the screen, jump to the first, last or any slide by number.

  • Next / Back
  • Swipe
  • Black screen
  • Go to slide

Live screen view

See the projected slide on your phone while you face the audience, with the laptop's pointer drawn on it. Only sent while you look, and always encrypted.

Talk timer

Set your talk length. The phone vibrates silently at 5 minutes, 1 minute and time up, and the clock turns amber, then red.

Touchscreen mode

Turn the phone sideways and the screen view becomes a touchscreen: tap to click, drag, pinch to zoom, two fingers to scroll.

Touchpad

Move, tap to click, two-finger scroll and right-click, hold to drag. Page up and down, zoom and fullscreen are one tap away.

Typing

Type into any box on the laptop with your phone's keyboard. Enter sends it, so searching takes one step.

Media

Play, pause, skip tracks and change the volume of whatever's playing. Rewind and forward in video players.

Tilt to point

Hold the pointer strip and move the phone like a laser pointer. Let go and the cursor stays where you left it.

Connect anywhere

Venue networks often stop devices from seeing each other. Kumpas gives you a way around every one of them.

Direct connection on your network. No internet needed.

Any network

Phone hotspot

Join your phone's hotspot from the laptop. Works anywhere.

USB cable

Plug in, turn on USB tethering, and you're connected.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth tethering for when there's no Wi-Fi at all.

Security

Nobody else on the Wi-Fi gets to drive your laptop

A remote that types on your computer has to be locked down. Kumpas was built that way from the first line, so a photo of your projected QR code, or someone on the same conference Wi-Fi, gets them nowhere.

Read the security FAQ

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Every tap, keystroke and screen picture is encrypted and authenticated with NaCl secretbox. Recorded traffic can't be replayed.

  • You approve every phone

    Pairing needs a click on the laptop and a matching 6-digit code on both screens. One paired phone at a time.

  • Single-use QR codes

    The pairing key never leaves your devices and is replaced after every attempt, so a photographed code is useless.

  • A fixed list of actions

    The phone can only trigger named actions like "next slide", never arbitrary commands. The relay passes along messages it can't read.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The remote runs in the phone's web browser, so any recent iPhone or Android phone works with Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Samsung Internet. Vibration alerts for the talk timer need a phone that supports vibration from the browser, which iPhones currently don't.

Do I need internet or an account?

No account, ever. Internet is optional: on the same Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot, a USB cable or Bluetooth tethering, everything stays between your two devices. The internet relay is off until you turn it on, and is only needed when your phone and laptop are on different networks. See the privacy policy.

Which presentation apps does it work with?

All of them. Kumpas presses keys on the laptop, the same way a USB clicker does, so PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, LibreOffice Impress, PDF viewers and web-based decks all respond.

Can someone else on the Wi-Fi control my laptop?

No. A phone has to be approved on the laptop with a matching code, and everything it sends is encrypted with a key only your two devices have. The QR code's key is single-use, so a photo of the code on the projector doesn't help anyone.

Can my phone see my screen without me knowing?

Screen pictures are only taken while the paired phone has the screen view open, the laptop's control panel shows when that's happening, and you can switch screen view off entirely.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

Early releases aren't code-signed yet. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. Signed installers are on the way: see the code signing policy.
